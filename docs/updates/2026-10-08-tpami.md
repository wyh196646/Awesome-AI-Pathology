# TPAMI pathology, cell-image and tissue-omics backfill

Date and search cutoff: **2026-10-08**. This update adds **23 publications**;
TPAMI grows from **5 to 28 entries**, and the collection grows from **3,607 to
3,630 entries**. The existing SCIE + CAS 2025 upgraded major-category zone-1
journal requirement continues to apply. No new journal was added to the allowlist.

## Search and screening

PubMed was queried separately for each complete TPAMI publication year from 2023
up to the cutoff. These queries deliberately contained no pathology title filter.
They returned 1,075 (2023), 793 (2024), 767 (2025), and 1,007 (2026) records.
A separate pre-2023 topic query returned 24 records. The queries overlap because
online and issue publication dates differ; after PMID deduplication there were
**3,586 unique records**, not the sum of the yearly counts.

Titles and abstracts were screened with histology, cytology, WSI, nuclei, cell
and tissue microscopy, cancer multimodal omics, spatial transcriptomics and
single-cell terms, plus generic learning/segmentation terms. This produced 191
initial candidates; a second pass for bioinformatics, fluorescence, multiome
and platform names recovered four additional candidates. Keywords identify
candidates, not eligibility. Actual experimental context determines inclusion.

A 4,255-record Crossref journal cache retrieved on October 4 provided a discovery
cross-check, but had no abstracts and could not establish topical relevance.
All 23 added DOIs were checked again against fresh IEEE-deposited Crossref
metadata and their PubMed records. The authors' code confirms fluorescence FMD
experiments for OTUR. For CMVAE, the indexed experimental excerpt of the
author-hosted publisher-version manuscript identifies RNA/ATAC single-cell
multiome PBMC and bone-marrow datasets; the full PDF download failed.

The [machine-readable audit](2026-10-08-tpami.json) records exact queries, URLs,
authors, DOI, volume/issue metadata, date decisions and the source for each entry.
Only bibliographic metadata and short relevance notes are saved; abstracts and
publisher PDFs are not redistributed.

## Exclusion examples and limits

Radiology-only PCRLv2 and knee/pelvis MR segmentation, retinal OCT angiography,
drug-synergy-only prediction, agricultural plant phenotyping, molecular cryo-EM
manifold learning and atomic/material microscopy were excluded. Likewise, neural
network memory cells, Visual Genome, mathematical “pathological” cases and merely
motivational genomics references do not establish relevance.

This is a verified backfill, not a claim that every IEEE full text has been
inspected. Indexing lag, incomplete abstracts and inaccessible manuscripts can
leave additional candidates unresolved. The three explicitly deferred papers
remain visible in this audit for later verification.
