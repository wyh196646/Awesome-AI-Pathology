# Journal cleanup: CAS 2025 major-category zone 1

Date: **2026-10-08**. Applies to every publication year, including the historical
entries in the README. This is a journal eligibility cleanup, not a new paper search.

## Rule and sources

Retain formal journal entries only when the journal is **SCIE indexed** and
**major-category zone 1 in the CAS 2025 upgraded edition**. All publication years
use this latest edition. Conferences, workshops, preprints and the existing thesis
entry keep their topical eligibility rules. JCR Q1, minor-category zone 1 and the
Top label are insufficient for journal inclusion.

The [September 24 institutional notice](https://kyc.hezeu.edu.cn/info/1040/4015.htm)
states that CAS stopped issuing editions from 2026 and its platform/API closed on
September 30. The latest available CAS edition is **2025**. The 2026 Xinrui table
is a separate product and is not used for this cleanup.

The rankings were read from the public
[Hebei Normal University 2025 CAS spreadsheet](https://huaxue.hebtu.edu.cn/a/2025/09/23/A5A5BBD96F6B4410B87B6D4C43BDFD63.html)
(21,772 rows, `Sheet1`, title/rank/Top columns). Journal names were matched exactly
after case, punctuation and ampersand normalization. Explicit aliases resolve
leading articles, PubMed subtitles, location qualifiers and renamed journals.
Fuzzy suggestions were not accepted as automatic matches.

SCIE membership and ISSNs were checked against the
[June 2025 institutional SCIE catalogue](https://lib.fjut.edu.cn/qkpj/list.htm).
Index membership was also checked in the
[2026 institutional Web of Science table](https://www.niic.nsc.ru/phocadownload/publications/2025/JCR_JournalImpactFactor_2026.pdf).
That table's JCR quartiles were not used to determine CAS ranks. For example,
Cell Genomics, Med, MedComm and The Innovation have CAS zone-1 records but are
listed as ESCI, so they do not meet the SCIE condition. eLife is also excluded
under the SCIE condition, consistent with its
[publisher's indexing statement](https://elifesciences.org/inside-elife/c11c6101/the-elife-model-an-update-on-progress-following-changes-in-web-of-science-indexing-status).

[The decision audit](2026-10-08-cas-zone1.json) records all 393 journal/series venue
labels, counts, matched source rows and exclusion reasons. The
[allowlist](../../config/journal-policy.json) records the 101 retained journal
titles and aliases, ISSNs, ranks and source evidence. Download checksums identify
the source snapshots; the complete third-party spreadsheets are not redistributed.

## Maintenance and verification

`scripts/journal_policy.py` rejects unknown or ineligible journal headings in the
active bibliography. `scripts/refresh_index.py` runs that check before changing
any totals or links. New journal titles require source evidence and an allowlist
update. A GitHub Actions workflow checks the policy, tests and regenerated index
on pull requests and pushes to main. Thirteen tests cover the journal gate,
historical README entries, conference and preprint exemptions, identity matching
and the existing retrieval safeguards.

The weekly collector flags previously removed formal publications by complete
identifiers and normalized-title hashes. Future screening checks formal publication
links, including previously unseen titles, and excludes ineligible journal versions.
An excluded paper must not be restored simply by placing it under a preprint heading.
A separately verified conference publication may qualify under the conference rules.

All retained publication lines, links and order were compared with the baseline
commit `64f61c90b4266bc4a9302c30b16c79562c453c62` and preserved exactly. Empty journal
sections were removed and annual/README counts and links regenerated. No preprint
retrieval checkpoints were advanced. The existing Monday 09:00 Asia/Shanghai task
retains its schedule and now follows the journal gate.

Older dated audits remain historical search logs, not active publication lists or
current inclusion recommendations. Removed entries can be recovered from Git history.
